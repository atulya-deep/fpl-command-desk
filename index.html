Set up your team

This desk projects the next five gameweeks, plans your transfers deadline by deadline, and simulates the result thousands of times. It needs a squad to work from.

Load a team by its FPL number

  1. Put your team number in the box. It is the number in the address when you view your own team on the FPL site: /entry/7561127/event/3.
  2. Press Open my team data. A tab opens showing your squad as raw data.
  3. Select all of it (Ctrl+A, then Ctrl+C) and paste it below.
Open my team data ↗

A note on live syncing: the FPL API sends no cross-origin header, so this page cannot read your team straight from your browser — no site can. A squad you build here is saved in this browser. To have a team synced automatically instead, put its id in config.json in the repository and the six-hourly job will do it.

How these numbers are made

Team attack and defence ratings come from this season’s expected goals, shrunk toward a squad-market-value prior — after only 2 rounds the raw numbers are far too noisy to trust on their own. Those ratings feed a Poisson fixture model that produces expected goals for and against in every remaining fixture, which in turn drives clean-sheet odds, attacking returns (scaled by each player’s expected goal involvement per 90, itself shrunk toward a price-and-position baseline), defensive-contribution points, save points and bonus. Nominated penalty, corner and free-kick takers carry an explicit uplift — badged PEN, CO and FK below. Availability is taken live from the official feed. Re-run py update.py after each deadline and this page rewrites itself.
