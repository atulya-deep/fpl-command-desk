Set up your team

This desk projects the next five gameweeks, plans your transfers deadline by deadline, and simulates the result thousands of times. It needs a squad to work from.

Load a team by its FPL number

Your number is in the address when you view your own team on the FPL site: /entry/7561127/event/4.

The FPL API refuses requests from other websites, so this goes through a public read-only relay (r.jina.ai). Only your team number travels, and it is already public on every league table you appear in. If the relay is busy, paste the data yourself instead.

  1. Press Open my team data. A tab opens showing your squad as raw data.
  2. Select all of it and copy — Ctrl+A then Ctrl+C on a computer, or long-press and choose Select all on a phone.
  3. Paste it into the box and press Load pasted data.
Open my team data ↗

A note on live syncing: the FPL API sends no cross-origin header, so this page cannot read your team straight from your browser — no site can. A squad you build here is saved in this browser. To have a team synced automatically instead, put its id in config.json in the repository and the six-hourly job will do it.

How these numbers are made

Team attack and defence ratings come from this season’s expected goals, shrunk toward a squad-market-value prior — after only 5 rounds the raw numbers are far too noisy to trust on their own. Those ratings feed a Poisson fixture model that produces expected goals for and against in every remaining fixture, which in turn drives clean-sheet odds, attacking returns (scaled by each player’s expected goal involvement per 90, itself shrunk toward a price-and-position baseline), defensive-contribution points, save points and bonus. Nominated penalty, corner and free-kick takers carry an explicit uplift — badged PEN, CO and FK below. Availability is taken live from the official feed. Re-run py update.py after each deadline and this page rewrites itself.
